## The rejects we will be tempted to undo (don't)

1. Adding a fifth home destination because settings "needs a tab"
2. Keep-colored cards to "look like a notes app"
3. Notion slash menu because it's "powerful"
4. Graph view in the pitch deck
5. Auth wall to "drive conversion"
6. Pull-to-refresh because "that's what apps do"
7. Sticker pack because Goodnotes makes money
8. Masonry before 120fps
9. Collaboration before undo works
10. AI home before the editor is beautiful

1. Shared-element open

3. Split FAB defaulting to a blank note in <1s
4. Widgets on day one of polish (if not day one of code, day one of "done")
5. Haptics mapped to meaning
6. Local-first
7. Paper, not white
8. Exhaustive sealed UI state
9. Dynamic color
10. Inbox (no filing required)
