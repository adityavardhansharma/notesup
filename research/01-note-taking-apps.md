Scope: public apps, Play Store volume apps, iPad ink apps, PKM tools, and GitHub repos including one-star toys. Score is **UI/UX usefulness to Notesup**, not market share.

Legend: **A** = accept pattern. **R** = reject as a primary direction. **F** = fragment only.

## Patterns that survived contact with 120 apps

### Accepted (appear in the best work)

1. **One-tap create.** Keep, Drafts, Apple Notes, Memos, Notally.
2. **Card as the note's face.** Craft, Zoho Notebook, Goodnotes covers, Flomo.
3. **Grid for browsing, list for scanning.** Keep + Apple + Samsung all offer both.
4. **Pin is a first-class sort, not a tag.** Keep, Easy Notes, Apple.
5. **Mixed vertical content** (text then image then text). Apple Notes, Craft, Notion.
6. **Focus / typewriter mode** for writing. Bear, iA Writer, Ulysses.
7. **Ink as a page or a block, not the OS.** Apple Notes, Penly, INKredible.
8. **Export Markdown + PDF.** Bear, Craft, Joplin, Markor.
9. **Local-first.** Obsidian, Anytype, Standard Notes, Notally.
10. **Capture first, organize later.** Drafts, note-gen, Memos, jrnl.

### Rejected (appear in the worst "powerful" work)

1. Notebooks → stacks → tags → shortcuts → shortcuts to notebooks. Evernote.
2. Graph view as home. Roam / early Obsidian marketing.
3. Databases as notes. Notion mobile.
4. Infinite canvas as the only editor. OneNote phone.
5. 12-item bottom navigation. ClickUp / Taskade.
6. Ads, sticker shops, coin economies. Freenotes / ColorNote.
7. AI as the first screen. Mem, Storyflow, Open Notebook.
8. Messenger-bubble notes.
9. Permission-hungry "notes" that want contacts and location on first launch.
10. Desktop PKM dumped onto a phone.

## Top 12 to keep on a moodboard

Craft, Apple Notes, Bear, Google Keep (structure only), NotallyX, Quillnote, Drafts, iA Writer, reMarkable, Things 3 (see file 02), Penly, Zoho Notebook covers.

## What Notesup is *not*, after this list

Not Notion. Not Obsidian. Not OneNote. Not Goodnotes. Not Keep.

It is the missing Android product that sits between **Keep's speed**, **Craft's blocks**, **Bear's type**, and **Apple Notes' mixed media**, wearing **Material Expressive**.
